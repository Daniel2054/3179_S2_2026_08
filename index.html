Lego Colours

LEGO pieces come in a wide range of colours, from familiar red, yellow and blue to less common options such as glow-in-the-dark. These colours give builders the flexibility to create everything from colourful characters to realistic buildings and landscapes.

From 2010 to 2025, black, white and grey consistently occupy the top three positions for piece counts. The rankings of other colour families vary each year, showing changes in their use across the LEGO catalogue. Use the year slider to explore these differences.
